更新日期：2026-09-17

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Cifra has no accounts and does not upload your calculations to our servers.

Theme, language, tool switches, history, and handwriting stay on this device.

Currency conversion uses a built-in table by default. Only when you tap Refresh does the app request public rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). That request goes over the internet; the service may process network information such as IP address, solely to return rates—not for ads or cross-app tracking. Provider: ExchangeRate-API.

Handwriting recognition runs on device. BMI, mortgage, and interest results are for reference only and are not medical, loan, or investment advice.

We do not use the advertising identifier and do not serve third-party ads.
